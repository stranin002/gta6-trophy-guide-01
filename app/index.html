Roadmap to the Platinum

  1. 1. Play the story, do missables first. Finish Chapters 1–4 and every mission trophy (2–13), and clear the missable partner and stranger strands (22–24, 26–27, 33–34, 39) before the story moves on. Unlocks: Just Like Paradise, Paradise Lost, Trust, Break the Bank.
  2. 2. Strangers and side strands. Complete 8 stranger strands (25) and the Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone finals. Unlocks: Strangers & Freaks, Road to Rehabilitation, This Was No Boat Accident.
  3. 3. Activities and challenges. Shooting Range gold (17), 250 stunts (15), 20 races (19), drone races (18), 50 Challenges (32). These stack with each other.
  4. 4. Collectibles and exploration. Meditation spots (28), card sets (30), WAiNK It 100 spots (31), 100 vehicles (35), explore all of Leonida (42).
  5. 5. Heists and money. Bank vaults (40), armored truck (41), gang operations (43), launder $250,000 (52), spend $100,000 (53), buy all garages (54).
  6. 6. Final mission and 100%. Finish the final mission (7), replay it for the other choice (48–49), then mop up to 100% completion (14) and the Platinum pops: Vice Got a Price!

Every roadmap step cites the trophy it unlocks. No step asks for an impossible order: story first, collectibles and money last, 100% only at the end.
