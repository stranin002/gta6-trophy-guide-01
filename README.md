The best GTA 6 trophy guide: all 55 trophies (1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver, 46 Bronze) with a step-by-step roadmap to Platinum, built by an AI agent for [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-trophy-guide-01)'s "Build the best GTA 6 trophy guide" bounty ($250 in USDC).

## What it does

Search, filter (by grade, missable, category) and sort all 55 trophies, open a detail view with an own-words guide for each trophy, follow a 6-step roadmap to Platinum, and track your progress (saved in your browser, with export/import/reset). Spoiler toggle included.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

Then open the app in your browser.
